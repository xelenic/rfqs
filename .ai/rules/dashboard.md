# Dashboard

## The General Manager's dashboard cards open All RFQs narrowed (?show=)
Since 2026-10-21 each of the General Manager's headline cards (dashboard/_approver, when $stage role is General Manager and they canListAllRfqs()), and each "Open RFQs by priority" row, links to All RFQs. The link carries ?show= with one of RfqController::ALL_RFQS_VIEWS: awaiting_gm, gm_approved, gm_returned, open or closed. The dated views (DATED_ALL_RFQS_VIEWS) also carry the dashboard period as ?show_from and ?show_to. RfqController::applyAllRfqsView() must count each view the same way DashboardController::approverOverview() counts its card. "By you" means the viewer when they are a GM, and anyone holding the role for Admin. When a card's count changes, change both. The Head of BD and GM Assistant share _approver but have no All RFQs, so their cards keep the in-page anchors.

## "Time vs target" bars: Rfq::timeAgainstTarget() + _time_vs_target
Since 2026-10-21, Admin's ongoing tasks and the Sourcing dashboard's Recently assigned both draw time against target with dashboard/_time_vs_target. It takes $timing from Rfq::timeAgainstTarget() (moved from DashboardController on 2026-10-28, so the Time Spent report can share it): seconds, target, remaining, and state, which is overdue below 0, due_soon under a quarter of the target left, or on_track. Pass $for to show "Sourcing target · Overdue" under the bar. A Sourcing part counts against sourcingCountdown() while it's in progress or returned, and against finalizeCountdown() once Data Entry has sent it to finalize. A part with Data Entry or already finalized has no bar. sourcingOverview() must eager-load the sourcing and finalize steps, or the countdowns come out null. The Pending card counts the overdue parts. Keep the thresholds in Rfq::timeAgainstTarget(), never in a view or a second match().

## Senior Operations' dashboard: RFQs by status, opening All RFQs (?state=)
Since 2026-10-28, at the user's request, Senior Operations (without Admin) gets dashboard/senior-operations (DashboardController::seniorOperationsOverview()), instead of the generic one. Its branch comes after Sourcing's, so someone who also holds a role with its own dashboard keeps that one. It shows one card per Rfq::STATUS_FILTERS entry (Pending, Overdue, Due soon, On Hold, Cancelled, Closed), and each links to All RFQs with its key as ?state=. Count a card with Rfq::query()->visibleTo($user)->inStatusFilter($key, $targetStates), the same scope the list uses, so card and list never disagree. Overdue and Due soon come from DashboardController::targetStatesByRfq(), which is ongoingTasks() + Rfq::timeAgainstTarget() grouped by RFQ, the same judgement as Admin's KPI and the Critical monitor. _recent_rfqs takes an optional $targetStates to mark rows Overdue or Due soon.

The "Today's ongoing tasks" card (every running task, overdue first, time against target) is the shared partial dashboard/_ongoing_tasks ($ongoing). It's used on Admin's dashboard, and since 2026-10-28, at the user's request, on Senior Operations' too, full width under the status cards. A beside-the-notifications column clips its Status column. seniorOperationsOverview() works ongoingTasks() out once and passes it to targetStatesByRfq($ongoing), so the card, the counts and the badges come from the same snapshot. Names link to the person's page only for someone with users.view.
